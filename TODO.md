## Pricing Advice for Single Clients

### Key Considerations:
- **Higher per-unit costs**: Single orders don't benefit from economies of scale
- **Premium service**: Include markup for personalized attention and smaller batch handling
- **Market positioning**: Research local market rates in target country (Cuba)
- **Profit margin**: Aim for 15-25% profit margin on single orders vs 5-10% on bulk

### Recommended Pricing Strategy:
1. **Base markup**: Add 20-30% to bulk pricing for single orders
2. **Service premium**: Additional 10-15% for custom requests
3. **Shipping transparency**: Clearly communicate two-payment system
4. **Competitive analysis**: Monitor local competitors' pricing

### Example Calculation:
- Bulk product cost: $20
- Single client markup: +$6 (30%)
- Service premium: +$3 (15%)
- Total product price: $29
- Shipping: $10 + $4/lb (paid separately)

## Shein vs Temu Cost Comparison

### Platform Fees:
- **Shein**: Higher transaction fees (8-15%), premium seller fees
- **Temu**: Lower fees (5-8%), growing seller program

### Shipping Costs:
- **Both**: Similar international shipping rates ($10-25 base + weight-based)
- **Temu**: Slightly lower rates, more competitive bulk shipping
- **Shein**: More established logistics, potentially faster delivery

### Product Categories:
- **Shein**: Fashion, accessories, home goods (broader range)
- **Temu**: Similar but more budget-focused, electronics, toys

### Business Model Fit:
- **Shein**: Better for established sellers with higher volume
- **Temu**: Better for new sellers, lower entry barriers, faster growth potential

### Recommendation:
Start with Temu for lower costs and easier entry, expand to Shein as business grows.
